## Overview

Troly is a marketplace and club for diecast collectors. From your first piece to the grail you've
been hunting for years, Troly brings your whole collection together in one place and tells you how
much it's worth, in your own currency. Collectors can showcase their garage, put pieces up for sale
or trade, and connect with other people who share the same passion for 1:64 scale cars.

## Key Features

- **Digital Garage** - Catalog every piece, organize it in folders (Tesla, Muscle Cars, Porsche...) and keep the total value of your collection private or public.
- **Sale, Trade or Display** - Each piece can be listed for sale, offered for trade, or shown for display only, so the catalog reflects how the community really collects.
- **Value in Your Currency** - Prices are converted on the fly with up-to-date exchange rates, so collectors in Mexico, Brazil or the US see the value in their own money.
- **Public Profiles** - Every collector gets a shareable profile with their location, interests, pieces in the garage and active listings.
- **Sellers and Memberships** - Collectors can apply to become sellers, and a Premium annual membership unlocks the full experience.

## Growth by Invitation

Troly launched as an invite-only club. New members enter with an invitation code or join a
waitlist, and existing members can invite friends. Referral campaigns, such as a Hot Wheels
giveaway, turned that invitation system into the main growth engine, backed by automated welcome,
Premium and reminder emails.

## Technical Stack

- **Frontend** - React with React Router, a Progressive Web App (PWA), dark and light mode, and full internationalization.
- **Backend** - Node.js and Express with MongoDB Atlas, indexed queries, database-side pagination and gzip-compressed responses.
- **Payments** - Stripe Checkout for the Premium annual subscription and Stripe Connect for sellers.
- **Storage and Email** - S3-compatible object storage with optimized thumbnails, and transactional email with bounce tracking.

## Why Troly

Diecast collecting is more than a hobby: it is a market where rare pieces gain value over time.
Troly gives collectors the tools to treat their collection as the asset it is, while keeping the fun
of hunting, showing and trading cars with a community that speaks their language.
